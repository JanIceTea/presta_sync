# Descriptions

## de-DE
A high quality 2010 Yi Wu Pu'er with a dry storage. This tea is fairly densely pressed, so it takes a few steeps to fully open up. Hence, the first steeps are quite light but do have soft mouthfeel.

The following cups are exposing a bit of an aged flavour with a floral note. Nothing is too prominent while sipping, though later the taste expands fully with a light astringency. 


**Taste**

Light aged flavour with a floral note


**Trees**

Blend of various ecologically grown bushes and trees


**Storage**

Kunming dry storage (probably has seen some more humid storage at some point)


**Origin**

Yiwu Mountain, Xishuangbanna, Yunnan, China


**Harvest**

Spring 2010


**Ingredients**

Camellia Sinensis Assamica - large leave


**Brewing**

For all our Puerh teas we recommend Gong Fu style brewing using 5-8g of tea on 100ml water. Rinse the tea briefly and then do repeating steeps (up to 6-12) of 10-20 seconds.
